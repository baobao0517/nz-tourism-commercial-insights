# Sources and provenance

**Primary period:** 1 July 2025 – 30 June 2026. Retrieved and compiled November 2026.

1. [MBIE International Visitor Survey Q2 2026 public microdata](https://www.mbie.govt.nz/immigration-and-tourism/tourism-research-and-data/tourism-data-releases/international-visitor-survey-ivs/international-visitor-survey-data-download) — weighted visitor expenditure, purpose, stay, visit status.
2. [MBIE IVS Q2 2026 top-line PDF](https://teic.mbie.govt.nz/assets/ivs/2026Q2%20IVS%20Top-line%20results.pdf) — published rounded national, Australian and US spend and national historical context.
3. [MBIE IVS methodology](https://www.mbie.govt.nz/immigration-and-tourism/tourism-research-and-data/tourism-data-releases/international-visitor-survey-ivs/international-visitor-survey-methodology) — variables, weights and exclusions.
4. [MBIE IVS survey quality](https://www.mbie.govt.nz/immigration-and-tourism/tourism-research-and-data/tourism-data-releases/international-visitor-survey-ivs/survey-quality) — survey sample and relative margins of error.
5. [MBIE IVS release materials](https://www.mbie.govt.nz/immigration-and-tourism/tourism-research-and-data/tourism-data-releases/international-visitor-survey-ivs/international-visitor-survey-data-release-materials) — national and market historical context.
6. [Stats NZ international travel June 2026](https://www.stats.govt.nz/information-releases/international-travel-june-2026/) — actual overseas visitor arrivals, distinct from the IVS survey population.
7. [Stats NZ Infoshare](https://www.stats.govt.nz/tools/stats-infoshare/) — future country-by-country historical series work.

**Provenance:** (i) original official MBIE survey microdata (not redistributed); (ii) derived, aggregated market estimates; (iii) separately published actual visitor arrivals, which are **not** interchangeable with IVS weights.
