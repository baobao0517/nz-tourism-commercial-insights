01 · Visitor purpose mix

100% stacked bars show the survey-weighted proportion of visitors within each origin market, not the raw sample percentages.

Australia: holiday 42.6%, visiting friends and relatives (VFR) 37.7%. China and the US are more holiday concentrated (74.5% and 69.0%). Small groups should not be overinterpreted.

02 · Repeat versus first-time visits

Repeat visitation provides evidence of market familiarity, but does not directly measure price sensitivity or willingness to pay.

04 · Where visitors spend their money

Compare the composition of estimated in-New-Zealand spending, and switch to mean category spend per visitor. A high percentage share does not necessarily mean higher spending in dollar terms.

Tour packages can contain accommodation, transport and activities already included in the package price: do not add the same spending twice. International airfares are excluded from IVS treated spending.

05 · Commercial actions worth testing

These are evidence-led opportunities, not demonstrated causal or profit-maximising strategies. Operator conversion, unit economics and margin should be measured before making investment decisions.

Australia

Volume & convenience

Segment holiday and VFR visitors; test accessible accommodation, dining and flexible experiences, especially for repeat visitors.

China

Bundles & shopping

Test convenient tourism bundles, suitable channel partnerships and shopping-linked experiences. Many visitors are travelling to NZ for the first time.

United States

Accommodation & experiences

Test multi-night stays and experience combinations. Higher observed accommodation and dining spend does not, on its own, prove a higher willingness to pay.

Methods & limitations

Source: MBIE International Visitor Survey, Q2 2026, microdata YE June 2026, respondent-level population_weight. Mean daily spend is the weighted mean of each visitor's treated_spend / no_days_in_nz, not total spending divided by total stay days. Charts use annual aggregated estimates; no identifiable survey microdata is redistributed.

Rounded published official national/Australian/US spending totals reconcile with independently computed aggregates. The complete Stats NZ country-by-country 2019/2026 arrivals comparison—especially China's exact recovery rate—has not been independently verified and is excluded from this dashboard. Survey errors increase in small subgroups; no bespoke subgroup confidence intervals are claimed.
